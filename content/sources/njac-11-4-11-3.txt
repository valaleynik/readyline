(a) The generally applicable requirements are as follows: 1. The insurer shall provide a Buyer's Guide to all prospective purchasers, prior to accepting the applicant's initial premium or premium deposit. However, if the policy for which application is made contains an unconditional refund provision of at least 10 days, the Buyer's Guide may be delivered with the policy or prior to delivery of the policy; and 2. The insurer shall provide a policy summary to prospective purchasers where the insurer has identified the policy form as one that will not be marketed with an illustration. The policy summary shall show guarantees only. Delivery of the policy summary shall be consistent with the time for delivery of the Buyer's Guide as specified in (a)1 above. iv. Unless otherwise requested, the policy data shall be provided for 20 consecutive years beginning with the previous policy anniversary.
