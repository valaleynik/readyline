b. Any policy, contract or related form filed with the commissioner for approval pursuant to this section shall be deemed approved upon the expiration of 60 days after the submission of the form unless disapproved in writing by the commissioner within that time. Any such disapproval shall be based only on the specific provisions of applicable statutes or regulations adopted by the commissioner, or guidelines published by the commissioner as of the effective date of this act, with regard to forms of that type. A disapproved policy, contract or related form may be resubmitted.
